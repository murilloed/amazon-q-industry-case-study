# Controlled Access

Access to the industrial archives may be considered only when all of the
following are documented:

1. written authorization from the software owner;
2. a defined research purpose and minimum required file scope;
3. applicable ethics or institutional approval;
4. a confidentiality and data-protection agreement;
5. secure storage, retention, and deletion procedures; and
6. a prohibition on onward distribution.

Requests should identify the intended analyses and explain why the public
synthetic sample and aggregate results are insufficient.

Repository maintainers may deny access when authorization, privacy, security, or
licensing conditions are not satisfied.
